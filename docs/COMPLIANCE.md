# Australian Business & Compliance Considerations

**This is not legal advice.** It is a research summary to brief the client and their lawyer/accountant. Categories: **Law** (a legal requirement, with my confidence noted) · **Practice** (recommended) · **Confirm** (needs a qualified professional or client decision). Checked 14 Oct 2026.

| Area | What applies | Category | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Privacy Act 1988 / APPs** | Small businesses (≤ A$3M turnover) are generally exempt, with exceptions (e.g. businesses that trade in personal information). **The exemption still exists as of Oct 2026.** The government agreed in principle to remove it, but the Tranche 2 exposure draft (Aug–Sep 2026) does not remove it. The **statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy** (from 10 Jun 2025) applies regardless of size. Stripe/Uber/Google terms also require a privacy notice. | Law (exemption status) + **Confirm** whether the client is covered + **Practice** | Comply with the APPs anyway: privacy policy, collection notice at checkout, data minimisation, security, access/correction, retention. Disclose that data is shared with the payment provider, Uber (courier), POS, email provider, and any hosting outside WA/Australia |
| **Notifiable Data Breaches** | Applies to APP entities | **Confirm** | Have a breach-response runbook regardless |
| **Australian Consumer Law (ACL)** | Consumer guarantees (acceptable quality, fit for purpose, matching description) can't be excluded. "No refunds" statements are misleading. Unfair contract terms (penalties since Nov 2023) | Law (high confidence) | Terms must not purport to exclude guarantees. Refund policy distinguishes faults (remedy) from change of mind. Lawyer to review T&Cs |
| **Price display** | The total price must be prominent. Delivery/extra fees disclosed before purchase | Law (high confidence) | Show the delivery fee and total before payment. GST-inclusive prices |
| **Drip pricing ban** | *Competition and Consumer Amendment (Unfair Trading Practices) Act 2026* passed Jul 2026, **commences 1 Jul 2027**: prominent disclosure of transaction-based charges with the base price, plus a general ban on unfair trading practices (manipulative design) | Law (passed, future commencement) | Design now to comply: no hidden fees, no pre-ticked add-ons, no fake urgency/scarcity |
| **Card surcharging** | RBA reforms: surcharges on eftpos/Mastercard/Visa debit, credit and prepaid **removed from 1 Oct 2026**. Amex, PayPal and UnionPay also removed surcharging. Service/booking/public-holiday surcharges are not card surcharges. Payment-method discounts still allowed | Law/scheme rule (verified on RBA FAQ) | No card surcharge on the website. If a public-holiday surcharge is used in store, decide whether it applies online and display it upfront — **Confirm** |
| **GST & tax invoices** | Business appears GST-registered (ABN record). Customers can request a tax invoice. Tax invoices > A$82.50 have extra content requirements (buyer identity/ABN if requested) `[from memory — confirm with accountant]`. Some food items may be GST-free (e.g. certain basic foods), but most prepared café food is taxable | **Confirm** with accountant | Receipts show ABN, "Tax invoice", GST amount. Accountant confirms each item's GST treatment |
| **Food safety registration** | Food business registration with the local government (City of Perth) under the WA Food Act 2008. Delivery adds transport considerations (temperature, packaging, time) | Law (presumed already done for the shop) | **Confirm** that online/delivery operations are covered by the current registration and food-safety plan |
| **Allergen information** | Food Standards Code: allergen declarations (Std 1.2.3). For food not in a labelled package, information must be displayed or **provided on request** (Std 1.2.1). Plain English Allergen Labelling terms are in force. Explicit online-ordering rules weren't found in this research | Law (general) + **Confirm** how it applies online (WA Health / City of Perth EHO) | **Practice:** show the mandatory allergens per item on the website (peanut, tree nut, milk, egg, sesame, fish, crustacea, mollusc, soy, wheat/gluten, lupin, sulphites), a cross-contact disclaimer, and "ask staff". Data must come from the kitchen and be re-approved whenever recipes change |
| **Dietary claims** | "Vegetarian", "vegan", "gluten free" are representations under the ACL. "Gluten free" has a specific Food Standards meaning | Law (ACL general) + **Confirm** | Use "gluten-free" only if it truly qualifies. Prefer "no gluten-containing ingredients, prepared in a shared kitchen" |
| **Spam Act 2003** | Commercial emails/SMS need consent, sender identification and a functional unsubscribe. Transactional order messages are generally not "commercial" if they contain no marketing | Law (high confidence) | Unticked opt-in at checkout, record consent (time/source). Keep confirmations free of promos unless consented |
| **Cookies & analytics** | No standalone cookie law in AU. The Privacy Act covers personal information collected via tracking (if covered). Overseas visitors (EU/UK) may expect consent | Practice | Use cookieless/privacy-friendly analytics at launch. If Meta/Google Ads pixels are added later → consent banner + privacy policy update |
| **Accessibility** | Disability Discrimination Act 1992 applies to goods/services, including websites. WCAG is the accepted benchmark (not itself law) | Law (DDA) + Practice | Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Run an accessibility audit before launch |
| **Payment security (PCI DSS)** | Card-scheme rules via the acquirer. Hosted fields keep scope minimal (SAQ-A) | Contractual | Use Stripe Elements / Square Web Payments only. Never log card data. Complete the annual SAQ-A if requested |
| **Data retention** | APP 11.2: destroy/de-identify personal information no longer needed (if covered). ATO: keep tax records 5 years | Law (ATO) + Practice | Retention schedule in DATABASE.md §4. **Confirm** with the accountant |
| **Uber Direct / Stripe / Google terms** | Contractual obligations: data use, prohibited items, attribution (Google), customer-support model | Contractual | Read the Uber Direct Service Terms/Order Form before go-live (not publicly linked) |
| **Photo copyright** | SmartShoot photos commissioned via Uber Eats. Website usage rights unclear | Law (copyright) | **Blocking for publication:** get written permission |
| **Business identity on site** | ABN display is good practice and expected on tax invoices. The `.com.au` domain requires an AU business connection (ABN) | Practice / registry rule | Footer: legal entity name + ABN |
| **Employment/staff accounts** | Staff personal data in the admin (names/emails). The employee records exemption may apply | **Confirm** | Minimal staff data. Remove access on staff exit |

## Documents the client needs from a qualified professional
1. Privacy Policy (and a short collection notice for checkout).
2. Website Terms of Use + Online Ordering Terms (payment, cancellations, refunds, delivery by third party, allergens disclaimer, liability per ACL).
3. Refund & cancellation policy consistent with BR-ORD-4/5.
4. Accountant sign-off: GST treatment per item, tax invoice format, record retention.
5. Food-safety/allergen sign-off from the business's food safety supervisor (and EHO if unsure).

A generic template privacy policy is **not sufficient**: it must reflect this site's actual data flows (Uber, payment provider, POS, email, analytics, hosting location).
