## 6. Competitor & context observations (limited)

- Perth has few dedicated Indian chai/street-snack cafés. Search results mostly show general cafés with chai lattes. Interstate chai brands (e.g. Dropout Chaiwala in Melbourne, Chai Walli in Sydney) show the category is growing. `[Third-party]`, low confidence. I didn't do a full local competitor audit.
- Commercial context: on Uber Eats, Chai Tapri pays a **30% marketplace commission** (Uber delivery) or **8%** (pickup). Uber's own **webshop** product charges **25% / 6%**. **Uber Direct** charges **A$10 + A$1.10/km ex GST** per delivery with no commission (S10, S11). A direct website mainly saves money on **pickup orders and larger delivery baskets** (see ARCHITECTURE cost notes). `[Verified]`
