## 3. Quality assessment

**Strengths**
- Technically excellent and **perfectly consistent**: same marble surface, same 45° angle, same high-key light, same props (cinnamon, star anise, bay leaf). A menu grid will look cohesive.
- Every core item is covered. At 5000 px they can be cropped tightly (square or 4:5) for detail views without losing quality.
- They show the real takeaway packaging, which sets honest expectations for delivery.

**Limitations (these matter for a premium, distinctive site)**
- **Catalogue style, not atmosphere.** They are Uber Eats compliance shots: bright, neutral, propped. They show no *tapri* feeling, no people, steam, pouring, hands, timber interior or night-time Northbridge energy. Used alone, the site will look like a delivery-app listing.
- **The four chai shots are nearly identical** (glass on marble). In a grid they read as repetition. Use one chai image and present variants as a list.
- The white marble does not match the venue (warm timber, black slats). The website shouldn't build its palette around marble white.
- 5:4 landscape only, with no portrait/vertical crops planned for mobile heroes. A 4:5 or 9:16 crop is possible from 5000×4000 but tight around the props.
- Small text over these images will fail contrast. Keep text off the photos.
